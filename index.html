Egyptian Rat Screw

A game of speed and slaps

Shuffled 1 time
How to play

Take turns playing cards face-up onto the pile. When a face card or ace is played, the next player must respond with another face card or ace within a limited number of chances (J = 1, Q = 2, K = 3, A = 4). Fail and the challenger wins the pile.

Drag your card to the pile to play. Click the pile to slap it! A wrong slap costs you a card.

Collect all 52 cards to win.
